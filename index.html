🕶️ The Double-Blind AI Paradigm • Why Mandroos Blind?

Inspired by the gold standard of Double-Blind Clinical Trials, Mandroos Blind eliminates the most critical flaw in medical AI: Self-Evaluation Bias. When an AI model audits its own extraction, it inherently favors its own logic—frequently overlooking subtle omissions and grading its own hallucinations as accurate.

Mandroos Blind breaks this loop by deploying an independent, cross-model AI Auditor that evaluates work it did not generate. Operating completely blind to the extractor's internal assumptions, it cross-examines the extracted dataset line-by-line against the original Trial PDF—eradicating confirmation bias, intercepting 100% of hallucinations, and locking your clinical evidence into a verified, publication-grade standard.
